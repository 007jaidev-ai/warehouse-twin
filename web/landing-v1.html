01The premise

Most of a picking day is walking, not picking.

A picker here fills an order of about 7 lines and walks 95 metres to do it. Across a month that is hundreds of kilometres of floor — and almost none of it adds value. Slot the shelves well and that distance falls, for free. The twin doesn't argue it; it replays the real orders on each layout and counts the metres.

The key finding

ABC — the textbook answer — saves only 2.5%. Orders are 7 lines long, so the picker still visits most aisles. Grouping products that are bought together saves 4–5× more.

Stack slotting + batching

Add smart batching (group waiting orders whose aisles overlap) on the best layout and the month's walk drops to 70 km — a 68% cut from 218.

Held-out check: layouts built only from orders before 22 Nov, tested on the final 766 unseen orders — Optimized still −13.9%. The gains carry to future orders.

05Inside the twin

More than a slotting toy.

Live optimizer

Simulated annealing on your layout in a Web Worker — 24,000 swaps in ~15 s. Racks recolour as it improves, with a convergence chart against ABC / Affinity / Optimized.

IoT sensor mode

The replay streams what real hardware would: RFID dock gates, UWB cart tags, pick-to-light lamps and barcode confirmations. Live feed on screen — download it as JSON Lines.

Team of pickers

1–6 pickers clear the backlog from one queue. A narrow aisle holds one cart; the next picker waits at the entrance. Measures congestion, not just distance — 4 pickers can lose 23% to queueing.

Smart batching

Each trip takes the next order plus the three waiting orders whose aisles overlap it most. Same orders, 12–15% less walking than batching in arrival order.

Heatmaps

Rack-visit and floor-traffic overlays on one sequential colour ramp — a product keeps its colour across every layout, slide and poster, so the eye tracks it.

Runs fully offline

Render-on-demand WebGL — 0 draw calls while idle — baked lighting and instanced geometry. The whole twin opens from a folder, no server, no network.

06Built to be trusted

The numbers hold up.

— deterministic

One routing engine

The floor is a graph; all-pairs shortest paths from Floyd–Warshall, each trip ordered by nearest-neighbour and improved with 2-opt. The same code scores layouts in the browser and the offline optimizer — the numbers match to the metre.

— validated

Tested on unseen orders

Layouts were built only from orders before 22 Nov, then scored on the final week they never saw. Optimized still saved 13.9% — the slotting generalises, it isn't fit to the past.

— stress-tested

Zero errors under abuse

Full month at 300× speed, layout and mode switching mid-replay, hostile URL params and corrupt save data, headless: 0 JS errors, and replay totals match the scoreboard exactly.
